    * **Function:** Visualizes the trajectory of thrown objects and ricochets before you throw/shoot.

    * **Weakness:** Requires "aim time" (you must hold the button for 0.5s to see the line).
    * **Upgrade Path:**
        * *Lvl 1:* Simple arc visualization.
        * *Lvl 2:* Ricochet visualization (shows the bounce angle).
        * *Lvl 3:* "Auto-Correction" (Slightly magnetizes thrown objects to the target).

4.  **Micro-Spectrometer** (Investigation) **[NEW]**
    * **Function:** Scans objects for chemical traces, fingerprints, or heat residue.
    * **ImSim Utility:** **Detective Work.** See which keypad buttons were pressed recently (heat signature) to guess the code. See invisible laser tripwires.
    * **Strength:** Unlocks paths without needing hacking skills (by finding the code).
    * **Weakness:** useless in direct combat; battery drains fast when active.
    * **Upgrade Path:**
        * *Lvl 1:* Heat residue (recent touches).
        * *Lvl 2:* Electronic flux (see invisible lasers/traps).
        * *Lvl 3:* Structural analysis (highlights weak walls you can punch through).

*Replaced: Smuggler's Compartment.*
*New Focus: Active defense and managing the "Fake Death" mechanic.*

1.  **Optical Camouflage** (Stealth)
    * **Function:** Bends light. Becomes less effective if you move fast.
    * **Strength:** Complete invisibility when standing still in shadows.
    * **Weakness:** Making noise (shooting/running) breaks the camo instantly.
    * **Upgrade Path:**
        * *Lvl 1:* 10s Duration. Static shimmer.
        * *Lvl 2:* 20s Duration. Can move crouched without breaking.
        * *Lvl 3:* 30s Duration + "Prism Flash" (blinds enemies nearby when it deactivates).

2.  **Syn-Lungs** (Environment)
    * **Function:** Filters toxins and allows underwater breathing.
    * **Strength:** Opens entire new routes (sewers, gas-filled vents) that other players cannot use.
    * **Weakness:** Provides zero protection against bullets or physical damage.
    * **Upgrade Path:**
        * *Lvl 1:* 2 min breath / Basic Gas filter.
        * *Lvl 2:* 10 min breath / Immunity to tear gas and nerve agents.
        * *Lvl 3:* "Hyper-Oxidation" (Sprint stamina does not drain while holding breath).

3.  **Second Heart** (The "Fake Death" System)
    * **Function:** Upon reaching 0 HP, you enter a "Downed" state. If you don't move, enemies think you are dead.
    * **ImSim Utility:** **Reset.** Use your "death" to let guards walk away, then stand up and stab them.
    * **Strength:** Prevents "Game Over." Great for aggressive play.
    * **Weakness:** High cooldown (can only use once per 10 minutes). If an enemy decides to "double tap" (shoot your head to be sure), you die for real.
    * **Upgrade Path:**
        * *Lvl 1:* Revive with 20% HP.
        * *Lvl 2:* "Play Possum" (Heartbeat stops on scanners so drones ignore you).
        * *Lvl 3:* "Adrenaline Surge" (Revive with Time Slow active for 5 seconds).

4.  **Kinetic Capacitor** (Energy Absorption) **[NEW]**
    * **Function:** A battery in your chest that charges when you take damage.
    * **ImSim Utility:** **Damage-to-Power.** You get shot -> Your battery fills -> You use that battery to power your Optical Camo or Cyberdeck.
    * **Strength:** Rewards aggressive play.
    * **Weakness:** If you don't get hit, you have no extra energy.
    * **Upgrade Path:**
        * *Lvl 1:* Absorbs 10% of damage as battery.
        * *Lvl 2:* Discharge (Release stored energy as a shockwave knocking enemies back).
        * *Lvl 3:* Overcharge (When full, your weapon damage doubles).

# ⚠️ **CYBERPSYCHOSIS SYSTEM – “OVERLOAD THRESHOLD”**

Your character has a mental “Humanity Core” embedded in their brain.
Every aug, upgrade, or heavy software package **adds system load** to this core.

When the load exceeds the safe threshold, your character begins to lose:

* **motor stability**
* **decision control**
* **identity cohesion**
* **emotional regulation**

This isn’t just a meter — it's **a progression of consequences**.
